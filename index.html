Kavrylo is built around one principle — get out of the way.

What makes it different

  • 01

    Send a message.
    That is the feature.

    Kavrylo does one thing. Text goes from you to them, instantly. No status games, no read receipts pressure, no algorithmic feeds. A conversation app that stays a conversation app.

  • 02

    Private by default,
    not by setting.

    End-to-end encryption is on for every conversation, always. There is no toggle. Your messages are not stored on our servers after delivery. We designed the architecture so that we cannot read your messages even if we wanted to.

  • 03

    No settings
    to configure.

    Open the app. Write to someone. That is it. We intentionally removed every configuration screen that did not make messaging better. If you are looking for integrations, bots, or plugins — we are not the right tool.
